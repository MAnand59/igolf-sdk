# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [anand.mayank59@gmail.com](mailto:anand.mayank59@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent possible.
